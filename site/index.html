Priority-Fee Opportunity Index (PFI)

PFI = dislocation(bps) × log-scaled volume × clock-factor (×1.6 when the reference market is closed). It ranks where immediacy is most valuable — i.e. where takers bid write-priority up toward the 8 bp cap and where a maker's free, un-outbiddable cancel-first protection is worth the most.
